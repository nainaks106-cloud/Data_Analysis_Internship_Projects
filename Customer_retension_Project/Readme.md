# Customer Engagement & Retention Analytics

## Project Overview
Analysis of 10,000 European bank customers to identify 
behavioral drivers of customer churn.

## Key Findings
- Inactive customers are 1.88x more likely to churn
- 2 product customers have lowest churn rate of 7.6%
- 772 premium customers lost to silent churn
- Germany churn rate is 2x higher than France and Spain
